2. Legal Bases for Data Processing

We process personal data lawfully and transparently under the following legal grounds pursuant to Art. 6(1) EU GDPR and Arts. 6, 30, and 31 of the Swiss revDSG: (a) Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To create and administer accounts, host competitive tournaments, compute match standings, manage brackets, process leaderboards, and deliver platform functionality requested by users; (b) Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / Art. 31(1) FADP): To safeguard server infrastructure, prevent automated bot spam and DDoS attacks via Cloudflare Turnstile and honeypot traps, enforce fair-play anti-cheat measures, optimize telemetry pipelines, and troubleshoot system diagnostics; (c) Compliance with Legal Obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): To satisfy commercial accounting, tax, regulatory reporting, and statutory data retention mandates under Swiss law; (d) Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): When you voluntarily transmit messages via our contact form or opt-in to specific optional features.

3. Comprehensive Categories of Personal Data Collected

Depending on how you interact with AREENA, we may collect and process the following categories of personal information:

A. Identity & Account Credentials

Usernames, display aliases, email addresses, cryptographically hashed passwords (salted with argon2/bcrypt), avatar images, account roles (administrator, tournament director, player), and multi-factor authentication metadata where configured.

B. Esports Telemetry, Match Data & Leaderboard Records

Tournament registrations, seedings, match history, wins/losses, skill metrics, ELO ratings, live bracket progress, game scores, spectator logs, and competitive rankings across integrated titles.

C. Technical, Device & Server Connection Logs

Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, browser version, operating system, device screen resolution, referring URLs, access timestamps, HTTP status codes, latency metrics, and diagnostic crash reports.

D. Security, Anti-Spam & Anti-Cheat Telemetry

Cloudflare Turnstile verification challenge tokens, honeypot spam bot interaction signals, rate-limiting telemetry, anomaly detection logs, and match integrity telemetry to prevent fraud, automated abuse, and unfair gameplay.

E. Communications & Customer Support Data

Full name, contact email address, subject line, message payload submitted through the website contact form, direct support correspondence, and Mailgun delivery receipt telemetry.

F. Partner Ecosystem & API Data Exchange

Tournament results and statistics shared with or received from authorized partner platforms (including TTStats at ttstats.ch, TTT-Organizer at tt-turnier.ch, and TT-Mobile at tt-mobile.ch) to maintain synchronized ranking and bracket tables, alongside API access tokens.

G. Cookies & Client-Side Local Storage

Strictly necessary session cookies ('areena_session' with HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite protection) and local storage keys ('areena_theme', 'areena_lang') used exclusively for zero-flash theme rendering and multilingual language persistence.

4. Cookies, Local Storage & Tracking Technologies

AREENA is engineered with privacy by design. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. We use an encrypted HTTP-only session cookie ('areena_session') strictly for authenticated administrative sessions. Local storage keys ('areena_theme' and 'areena_lang') store your visual preferences on your device to ensure instant, zero-flash rendering on subsequent visits without sending tracking tokens to external servers. Cloudflare Turnstile operates as a non-intrusive, privacy-preserving CAPTCHA mechanism without setting persistent tracking cookies.

5. Sub-Processors, Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We share personal data strictly with trusted service providers under robust Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) compliant with Swiss and European standards: (1) Cloud Hosting & Server Infrastructure: ISO 27001-certified data centers located in Switzerland and the European Economic Area (EEA); (2) Transactional Email (Mailgun Technologies / Sinch): Utilized under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to deliver contact form messages and administrative alerts; (3) Edge Security & DDoS Mitigation (Cloudflare, Inc.): Utilized for network firewalling and Turnstile challenge verification; (4) Telemetry Badging (Uptime Kuma): Powers real-time platform telemetry at status.areena.ch; (5) Partner Platforms (TTStats, TTT-Organizer, TT-Mobile): Synchronized tournament schedules, rankings, and match telemetry; (6) Statutory Authorities: Disclosed only when strictly required under enforceable Swiss court orders or statutory law.

6. International Data Transfers & Cross-Border Safeguards

All primary data storage and server processing take place within Switzerland and the European Union. In instances where third-party processors (e.g., Mailgun or Cloudflare) transfer or process data in third countries, we ensure appropriate safeguards pursuant to Art. 44 et seq. GDPR and Arts. 16-17 Swiss FADP. These include Swiss Federal Council adequacy decisions, European Commission Adequacy Decisions, participation in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and standard contractual clauses (SCCs) supplemented by technical encryption.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to satisfy statutory obligations: (a) Account data is retained for the active lifespan of the account plus 10 years for statutory commercial record-keeping under the Swiss Code of Obligations (Art. 958f OR); (b) Security logs and IP records are automatically purged or anonymized within 30 to 90 days; (c) Contact form inquiries are retained for up to 12 months after resolution; (d) Tournament history and match records are permanently archived in aggregated form to ensure historical integrity and public leaderboard consistency.

8. Technical & Organizational Security Measures (TOMs)

AREENA implements state-of-the-art security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure: TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit with strict HSTS policies, salted password hashing algorithms (argon2/bcrypt), strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), principle of least privilege, automated encrypted database backups, isolated network tiers, and continuous automated vulnerability assessments.

9. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Swiss FADP (revDSG) and the EU GDPR, you are entitled to the following enforceable privacy rights without fee:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR / Art. 25 FADP): Obtain confirmation of whether your data is processed and receive a comprehensive copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR / Art. 32 FADP): Request the immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure / 'Right to be Forgotten' (Art. 17 GDPR): Request the permanent deletion of personal data when processing is no longer required or lawful.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): Limit the scope of data processing under applicable statutory conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR / Art. 28 FADP): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (JSON/CSV) or request its direct transfer to another controller.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): Object at any time to data processing carried out based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke previously granted consent at any time with future effect.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a formal complaint with the competent supervisory authority: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC / EDÖB), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, Switzerland (www.edoeb.admin.ch), or your relevant EU/EEA national Data Protection Authority.
