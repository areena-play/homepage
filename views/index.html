ABOUT AREENA

Transforming Sports Association Management

AREENA consolidates fragmented spreadsheets, disconnected legacy portals, and manual administrative overhead into a modern, cloud-native Single Source of Truth.

🗄️

Single Source of Truth

Unified database architecture eliminating data silos, redundancy, and error-prone spreadsheet work across the entire association.

🏆

Automated League & Cup Engine

End-to-end automation for national and regional leagues, cup brackets, round-robin stages, and live standings calculation.

🪪

Licensing & Identity Management

Comprehensive digital player passports, referee and coach qualifications, license levels, and secure identity verification.

🔌

Open API & Ecosystem Integrations

API-first architecture enabling third-party developers, companion apps, club software (e.g. Clubdesk), and accounting tools to connect seamlessly.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Sports Federations & Clubs

Role-Specific User Portals

Tailored interfaces for players, team captains, club admins, federation officials, and fans on desktop and mobile.
