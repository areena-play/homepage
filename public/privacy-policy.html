Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview & Data Controller

AREENA ('we', 'us', or 'our') respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect account details, gaming telemetry (match history, rankings), communication records when you contact us, and technical log data (IP address, browser type) necessary for platform integrity and security.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide matchmaking, orchestrate tournaments, prevent cheating, communicate critical service updates, and optimize platform performance.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

You retain full rights to request access, correction, data portability, or erasure of your personal records. Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@areena.play.
